Gustafson: E(P) = 1 − α(P − 1)/P = 1 − α + α/P, so lim E(P) = 1 − α as P→∞. Efficiency stays bounded away from zero.
Amdahl: E(P) = S/P = 1 / (sP + 1 − s), so lim E(P) = 0 as P→∞, because the denominator grows linearly in P.
Takeaway: under weak scaling efficiency stabilizes at 1 − α, while under strong scaling it vanishes.
